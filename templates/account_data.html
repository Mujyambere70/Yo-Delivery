Delete my account

Your name, contacts, addresses, cart, wishlist, notifications and messages are removed, and you cannot sign in any more. Orders, tickets and payments stay without your name, because a shop must keep its invoices.

Before deleting: your Yo Pay balance must be zero and no order, parcel or trip may be on the way.
