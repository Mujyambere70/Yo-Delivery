The charge is taken from the ticket price; the convenience fee ({{ 2000|rupees }} per passenger) is not refunded. When the operator cancels a trip, you get all your money back. Refunds to Yo Pay arrive at once.

Good to know

  • Booking closes {{ CLOSE.bus }} minutes before a bus, {{ CLOSE.train }} minutes before a train and {{ CLOSE.flight }} minutes before a flight.
  • One booking can have up to {{ MAX_PAX }} passengers. Every passenger has a seat.
  • Bus and flight fares go up when the trip fills up; train fares are fixed.
  • Times are local: IST in India, the local time of the city abroad.
  • No airport in your town? We show the flights from the nearest airport.
  • You get Yo Rewards points after the trip: 1 point for every {{ 10000|rupees }}.

Yo Tickets is part of a demo project: the bus, train and airline companies are fictional and no real tickets are sold. Some seats are already taken by "other travellers" so the seat maps look real.
