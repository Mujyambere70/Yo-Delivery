How it is calculated

Monthly payment E = P × r × (1 + r)n ÷ ((1 + r)n − 1), where P is the amount, r the interest per month (yearly rate ÷ 12 ÷ 100) and n the number of months.

No-cost EMI (3 and 6 months, from {{ NO_COST_FROM|rupees }}): the shop pays the interest, so you pay only the price.
