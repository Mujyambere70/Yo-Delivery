Transport companies publish their timetables in the open GTFS format: a ZIP file with the tables agency, stops, routes, trips, stop_times and calendar. Every stop is matched to a town or a village of our list; every trip becomes services for each pair of its stops, with the real times, days and dates. For a route with a real timetable, Yo Tickets shows only the real trips.

Fares follow the rules of Yo Tickets in this version (the fare tables of the company are not read). Live positions (GTFS-Realtime) need a contract with the company.
