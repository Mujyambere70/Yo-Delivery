The route plan of a courier needs a speed to say when he arrives at every stop. Version 7 used {{ fixed }} km/h everywhere. Hours without enough points use the value of the whole day, and towns without data the fixed {{ fixed }} km/h.

It is also counted once a day by the job queue.
