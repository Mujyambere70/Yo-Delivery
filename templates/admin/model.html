How it works

Matrix factorization for implicit feedback (alternating least squares, NumPy): every customer and every product gets 16 numbers learned from views (1), wishlists (2) and purchases (3). Before it is used, one product of each customer is hidden and the model must find it in its top 10 more often than the list of the most popular products. Otherwise the shop keeps the simple rules. It trains every day by itself.

🖼 Similar products from texts and pictures

For every product the shop finds the closest products by the words of its name and description (TF-IDF) and by its main picture (colours and shapes). New products are recommended from the first day, before anybody buys them. The page "Similar products" mixes these with the learned model when the model knows the product.
