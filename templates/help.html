Returns & replacement

Most items can be replaced within 7 days of delivery if they are damaged, defective or different from the description. Grocery items are non-returnable.

About Yo Delivery

Yo Delivery started as a fast local delivery service in Ambala, Haryana and grew into an online store for electronics, fashion, footwear, watches, grocery, home essentials and books.

This website is a demo built with Python (Flask), HTML and CSS for an academic project. All products, brands and sellers are fictional.
