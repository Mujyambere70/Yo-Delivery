Returns & refunds

You can return a delivered order within {{ SETTINGS.return_days }} days: open the order and press "Return this order", then choose the reason. A courier picks up the parcel; after our check you get the money back — to your card for card payments, otherwise to your Yo Pay wallet. You get a message when the refund is sent.

About Yo Delivery

Yo Delivery started as a fast local delivery service in Ambala, Haryana and grew into an international online store and marketplace for electronics, fashion, footwear, watches, grocery, home essentials and books. More about us and the developer ›
