Couriers and workers of Yo Delivery show who they are once. It protects our customers - and you.

What we do with your photos

  • You send a photo of your ID document (the page with your portrait) and a selfie taken now.
  • A computer finds the face on both photos and tells our staff how alike they are. A person always decides, never the computer alone.
  • If you add the two lines at the bottom of your passport (MRZ), we check their check digits and the expiry date.
  • We keep only: the kind of document, the country, the expiry date, the last 3 characters of the number and the result. The photos are deleted {{ RETENTION }} days after the decision.
  • Only staff with the ability "check courier photos and private files" can see them. You can take back your consent and delete the photos at any time on this page.
